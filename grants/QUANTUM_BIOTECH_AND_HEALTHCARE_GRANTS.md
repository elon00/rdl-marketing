## 1. 🦟 Gates Foundation Grand Challenges (Malaria & Tuberculosis Track)

- **Target Portal:** [Gates Foundation Grand Challenges](https://grandchallenges.org/)
- **Project Title:** Quantum Variational Eigensolver (VQE) & Quantum Machine Learning for Accelerated Lead Optimization Against Drug-Resistant Malaria & Tuberculosis
- **Principal Lead:** `elon00` (QMoosa Life Sciences & Deep Tech Core)
- **Primary Repository:** [`elon00/Quantum-Drug-Discovery-for-Malaria-Tuberculosis-Using-VQE-QML`](https://github.com/elon00/Quantum-Drug-Discovery-for-Malaria-Tuberculosis-Using-VQE-QML)

### Executive Summary
> "Tuberculosis (TB) and Malaria claim millions of lives annually, exacerbated by emergent multi-drug resistance against artemisinin- and rifampicin-based regimens. Classical molecular docking struggles with high-dimensional electronic correlation calculations in transition metal cofactor binding sites.
>
> Our open-source platform deploys the **Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE)** coupled with parameterized **Quantum Machine Learning (QML)** circuits (using Qiskit and statevector simulation) to compute exact ground-state energies and binding affinities for novel inhibitor candidates at quantum-chemical precision."

### Milestone Roadmap
- **Milestone 1 ($50,000):** Mapping target enzyme active site Hamiltonians (e.g., *Plasmodium falciparum* dihydrofolate reductase and *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* InhA) into qubit representation via Jordan-Wigner and Parity transformations.
- **Milestone 2 ($75,000):** VQE energy surface optimization and hardware-efficient ansatz testing on IBM Quantum hardware backends.
- **Milestone 3 ($50,000):** Hybrid QML classifier training on small-molecule libraries for pharmacokinetic bioavailability prediction (ADMET).

## 2. ⚡ Wellcome Leap Quantum for Bio (Q4Bio)

- **Target Portal:** [Wellcome Leap Q4Bio](https://wellcomeleap.org/q4bio/)
- **Core Focus:** Developing scalable quantum algorithms to simulate biological macromolecules that are intractable on classical high-performance compute clusters.
- **Technical Proposition:**
  - Hybrid classical-quantum active space reduction (CASSCF + VQE).
  - Quantum error mitigation strategies (Zero-Noise Extrapolation, Readout Mitigation) applied to target metalloprotein complexes.
